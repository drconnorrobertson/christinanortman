About

Strategic Tax Advisory, Not Reactive Compliance

A CPA who builds proactive strategies for business owners and investors ready for more than year-end surprises.

Christina Nortman is a Certified Public Accountant and Managing Partner of the Northeast Region at AE Tax Advisors, based in Maryland. She brings the rigor and analytical discipline cultivated at PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) and CohnReznick to every client engagement.

Christina works exclusively with high-income business owners and real estate investors who have outgrown reactive tax preparation. Her approach combines deep technical expertise with forward-looking strategy, ensuring clients are positioned to minimize their tax burden legally, efficiently, and sustainably.

"Tax planning should happen before decisions are made, not after the year is over."
