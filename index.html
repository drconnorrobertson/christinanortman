About

Big Four Rigor.

Christina Nortman is a Certified Public Accountant and Managing Partner of the Northeast Region at AE Tax Advisors. Based in Maryland, she brings the analytical rigor and institutional-grade methodology cultivated at PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) and CohnReznick to every client engagement.

Christina works exclusively with high-income business owners and real estate investors who have outgrown reactive tax preparation. Her approach combines deep technical expertise with forward-looking strategy, ensuring clients are positioned to minimize their tax burden legally, efficiently, and sustainably.
